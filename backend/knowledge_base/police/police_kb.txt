POLICE REPORTING & FIR GUIDELINES:
1. For theft or loss of identity documents and mobile phones, file an online Lost Article Report or visit the nearest police station having local jurisdiction over the incident location.
2. Ensure you obtain an official FIR copy or stamped acknowledgment receipt with a unique complaint reference number.
3. An official police report or Loss Certificate is mandatory for requesting duplicate Driving Licences, blocking stolen mobile IMEI numbers, and preventing fraudulent misuse of lost documents.
