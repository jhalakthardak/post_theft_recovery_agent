BANK CARD BLOCKING & FINANCIAL PROTECTION:
1. Immediately contact the customer service hotline of the issuing bank to freeze all debit and credit cards.
2. Log in to official internet banking or mobile banking app to temporarily disable international and online card transactions.
3. Review recent transaction history for unauthorized charges and immediately register a dispute with the bank for any fraudulent withdrawals.
4. If UPI or net banking access is enabled on a stolen mobile device, call the national cybercrime helpline (1930) to freeze connected payment handles.
